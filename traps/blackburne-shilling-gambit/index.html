Blackburne Shilling Gambit

Black hangs a pawn on purpose. Grab it, get greedy, and it's mate on move seven.

In plain words

Black leaves a pawn hanging on purpose. If White grabs it and then goes for even more, Black's queen crashes in and a knight gives checkmate. Don't take the pawn: castle, or take Black's knight.

The trap

How the Blackburne Shilling Gambit works

After 1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bc4 Nd4?! the e5 pawn is loose. 4.Nxe5? Qg5! hits the knight and g2, and after the greedy 5.Nxf7?? Qxg2 6.Rf1 Qxe4+ 7.Be2 Nf3# is mate. Simply 4.O-O or 4.Nxd4 is good for White.

How not to fall for it

  1. Don't take on e5. Castle, or take the knight on d4.
  2. If you did take and the queen comes to g5, don't grab more: castle (your king then covers g2) and give the knight back.
  3. A free pawn that opens lines to your king is not free.
