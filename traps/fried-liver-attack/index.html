Fried Liver Attack

White gives up a knight on f7 and drags Black's king into the middle of the board.

In plain words

White sacrifices a knight to pull Black's king out of its safe spot. The king ends up in the middle of the board, where every white piece can attack it. Avoid it: don't take back in the center, attack the bishop with your knight instead.

The trap

How the Fried Liver Attack works

In the Two Knights Defense (1.e4 e5 2.Nf3 Nc6 3.Bc4 Nf6) White plays 4.Ng5 and attacks f7. After 4…d5 5.exd5 the natural 5…Nxd5? allows 6.Nxf7! Kxf7 7.Qf3+ Ke6 8.Nc3 and Black's king is stuck in the center. The fix is 5…Na5!

How not to fall for it

  1. Don't take back on d5 with the knight. Play 5…Na5! and attack the bishop.
  2. A king in the center is a target. Keep it behind its pawns.
