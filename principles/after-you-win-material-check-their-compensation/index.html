Principles · Middlegame plan

After you win material, check their compensation

IntermediateMiddlegame
Ask what the opponent got in return, for example the bishop pair. Then trade off that specific advantage.

Tip: use the arrow keys. Click a piece and a square to try your own moves.

Black to move (move 22). Black is a pawn up; White's compensation is the two bishops. 22...Nbxd3 trades a knight for one of them, and after cxd3 Nxd3 Black also wins a second pawn. Ask what the opponent got in return, then trade that exact asset. It is the strongest move, and Black is much better.
Source: Van Foreest, Jorden vs Gukesh, D, Tata Steel Masters 2024, 2024 (https://lichess.org/broadcast/tata-steel-masters-2024/round-6/ubgeWaHz/hRafgim5)
